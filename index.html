Who We Are

The voice of SIIB, out in the open.

The Public Relations & Media Committee is the storytelling engine of Symbiosis Institute of International Business. We capture campus life, cover institute events, and carry the achievements of students and faculty to the world.

From day-to-day announcements to flagship campaigns, we manage every official channel — making sure the SIIB story is told with clarity, consistency, and craft.
